Melodic hard rock supergroup **Kings Of Mercia** have unleashed **"Aftermath"**, the second single from their upcoming sophomore effort, **Battle Scars**.

The track is an outstanding showcase of the band's growth and chemistry, featuring an incredible performance by guitarist Jim Matheos, vocalist Steve Overland, bassist Joey Vera, and drummer Simon Phillips.

Listen to **"Aftermath"** on all major streaming platforms now!

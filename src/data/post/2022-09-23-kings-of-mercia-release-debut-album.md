Today marks the release of the highly anticipated self-titled debut album by **Kings Of Mercia** via **Metal Blade Records**!

Brought together by **Jim Matheos** (Fates Warning) and **Steve Overland** (FM), the band features a stellar rhythm section with **Joey Vera** (Armored Saint, Fates Warning) on bass and **Simon Phillips** (ex-Toto) on drums. The result is a stunning blend of melodic hard rock, big riffs, and rich vocal harmonies.

### Track List:

1. Wrecking Ball
2. Humankind
3. Sweet Revenge
4. Set the World on Fire
5. Too Far Gone
6. Liberate Me
7. Nowhere Man
8. Everyday Angels
9. Is It Right?
10. Your Life

Order the album now at [www.metalblade.com/kingsofmercia](https://www.metalblade.com/kingsofmercia).

The wait is finally over! **Kings Of Mercia**'s sophomore album, **Battle Scars**, is officially out today via **Metal Blade Records**!

Expanding on the foundations laid by their debut, **Battle Scars** delivers ten tracks of premium hard rock. With Jim Matheos's heavy guitar riffs, Steve Overland's soaring vocals, Joey Vera's thick bass lines, and Simon Phillips's legendary drumming, the album is a must-listen for progressive and melodic rock fans alike.

### Track List:

1. Guns and Ammunition
2. Eye for an Eye
3. Between Two Worlds
4. Legend
5. Battle Scars
6. Don't Ask
7. Aftermath
8. Hell 'n' Back
9. Cold
10. Angels & Demons

Get your copy now at [www.metalblade.com/kingsofmercia](https://www.metalblade.com/kingsofmercia).
